01 / THE METHOD

A score with a story.

MOANIIMO calculates its score using BREAK stat, skill and ultimate power per second, and applicable BREAK multipliers. Unconditional traits are included.

The model uses a level 60 build and does not account for enemy resistance, positioning or hit count. Conditional effects are shown separately, so your results may differ.
