Reading the atlas

Numbers show purchase order. The center is free. Nodes connect along their sides, never diagonally. Start with any board, then follow its numbered route.

Select a node, use the previous and next buttons, or drag the slider. The step filter dims later purchases. Arrow keys work when a map node has focus.

Jade marks active skills, lilac marks passives, and gold marks corner bonuses. Skill totals use a base level of 10.

The 149-point builds and Azphel at 40 points include exact routes. The 316-point builds and Azphel at 100 points only provide aggregate totals, so no routes are drawn for those budgets.

Azphel uses separate crystals. Its 40-point and 100-point budgets are examples. The source does not list individual Azphel node costs.

Priority weights are the author's scoring inputs, not measured healing or damage.
