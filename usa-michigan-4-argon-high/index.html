98th Meridian & US Macro-Regions

The 98th meridian roughly follows the 51 cm (20-inch) annual rainfall line from North Dakota through Texas. East of this line, moist Gulf of Mexico air supports dense rain-fed agriculture, navigable rivers, and 80% of the US population. West of the line lies the arid rain shadow of the Rocky Mountains.

Key Geographic Takeaway

Understanding the Mississippi Basin, Great Lakes shipping corridor, and 98th meridian explains why industrial cities like Chicago and Detroit grew where they did.
