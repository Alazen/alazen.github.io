01How are the Aniimo ranked?

By default, we sort the 26 Break-role Aniimo by base BREAK, highest first. Equal BREAK values use total attributes as a tie-breaker, then Aniilog number. Use the Rank by control to explore another stat. Rank numbers refer to the full roster before filtering.

02Why is Squashel ahead of Inferlupa?

Both have 108 base BREAK. Squashel has 548 total attributes and Inferlupa has 541, so Squashel wins the tie-break. That small difference does not settle which skill kit suits your team.

03Which forms and stages are included?

All 26 Basic Form entries assigned to the Break role in the official Aniimo wiki. Lumin, Gamma, and Nova stages are included, and you can filter by stage. Alternate and Prismana forms have their own entries and are outside this index.

04Where do the stats come from?

Each creature's official wiki entry, checked on 12 October 2026. Open any creature to follow its source. Consult the official wiki for each creature's skills, alternate forms, and later balance changes.
